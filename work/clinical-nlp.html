01 / TECHNICAL CHALLENGE

How can we extract consistent information from multilingual text when labelled data is limited and errors have different costs?

02 / METHOD & PIPELINE

These projects combine transformer-based language models, active learning and task-specific rules. The methods address entity detection, limited annotation budgets, terminology variation and the conversion of free text into normalised records.

  1. 01Unstructured text
  2. 02Detect & extract
  3. 03Normalise & validate
  4. 04Structured records
03 / RESULTS & EVALUATION

ClinicAno’s hybrid configuration achieved 0.875 recall and 0.858 F1 on 50 held-out notes. MEDDOCAN alone achieved the higher F1 of 0.881, making the precision–recall trade-off explicit. Related score-extraction workflows standardised information for downstream analysis.

Evaluation scope

Results belong to the collaborative studies and their evaluation settings. The hybrid approach prioritised recall; performance should be re-evaluated for new document types and domains.

04 / MY CONTRIBUTION

I am a co-author on four team contributions covering ClinicAno, genomic entity recognition, APGAR extraction and ECOG standardisation. They connect NLP methods with repeatable data-processing workflows.

05 / APPLICATION & TRANSFERABLE SKILLS

The source documents are Spanish and Catalan clinical notes. Tasks include de-identification, genomic entities, and APGAR and ECOG scores, with outputs prepared for clinical research and OMOP-based analysis.

What this work demonstrates

Multilingual NLP, information extraction, data-centric model adaptation, human-in-the-loop annotation and automation of repeated text-processing tasks.
