02 / ABOUT ME

A builder’s mindset.
Room to go further.

I work on machine learning models and the data workflows around them: preparing inputs, fine-tuning models, evaluating outputs and making the process reproducible. I also build applications and explore ways to automate processes.

My degree in Bioinformatics and current MSc in Health Data Science add a valuable specialisation. Biomedical datasets have given me experience with limited labels, heterogeneous sources, complex relationships and demanding validation requirements.

At Helmholtz Munich and in the PRBB research environment, I have applied these skills to imaging, relational data and multilingual text. I’m looking to bring that technical foundation to applied AI, data science and data-platform teams across industries.
